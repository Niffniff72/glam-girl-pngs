# Mini Drama: "The Cookie Shop Mix-Up"

A 20–25 second funny, not-salesy reel for the "I've Got a Lot on My Plate (and it's mostly cookies)" tee.
The shirt causes the moment, and nobody pitches it. Viewers ask "where'd you get that shirt?" on their own.

Make 4 pictures in Nano Banana (upload your cookie-tee picture each time), then animate each one.

**Add this to every picture prompt:**
> Keep the exact same woman: same face, side-swept bangs, curly ponytail, gold hoop earrings, red Christmas t-shirt that says "I'VE GOT A LOT ON MY PLATE RIGHT NOW (and it's mostly cookies)", black joggers, white sneakers. Same illustrated style. Show the full shirt text clearly. Vertical 9:16.

**Add this to every animation prompt:**
> Keep her face, hair, earrings, red cookie t-shirt and the bakery exactly the same. Keep all text unchanged. Small, smooth movements. No smoke or steam.

---

## Scene 1: She walks in (about 5 sec)
**Picture:** She walks into a cute pink-and-cream bakery with cookie display cases, holding up one finger to the friendly baker behind the counter.
**Animate:** She walks up to the counter smiling and holds up one finger like she's ordering just one cookie.
**Caption:** "Just ONE cookie, please 🍪"

## Scene 2: The baker reads her shirt (about 5 sec)
**Picture:** Close-up of the friendly baker behind the counter, wearing an apron, looking at her shirt with one raised eyebrow and a smirk.
**Animate:** The baker reads her shirt, raises an eyebrow, and slowly smiles.
**Caption:** *(none, let the face do the talking)*

## Scene 3: The big platter (about 5 sec)
**Picture:** The baker hands her a huge platter piled high with Christmas cookies over the counter. She looks surprised and delighted.
**Animate:** She takes the big platter, looks down at her shirt, looks at the camera, and shrugs with a big laugh.
**Caption:** "…the shirt said what it said 😂"

## Scene 4: The happy ending (about 5 sec)
**Picture:** She walks out of the bakery door happily holding the big cookie platter, winking at the camera.
**Animate:** She walks toward the camera holding the platter, takes a bite of a gingerbread cookie, and winks.
**End card text:** "Grab yours, sis / SHOP THE COOKIE TEE / LINK IN BIO 🍪"
