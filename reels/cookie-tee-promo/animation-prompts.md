# Animation Prompts: Digital Dream Gal Haven Character

Use with any image-to-video app (Kling AI, Hailuo, Pika, Meta AI).
Upload the Digital Dream Gal Haven picture as the starting image, paste a prompt, and choose **5 seconds** and **9:16 (vertical)**.

Always add this to the end of every prompt:
> Keep her face, hair, hoop earrings, pink outfit, white sneakers and the pink office exactly the same. Smooth, natural, realistic movement. 2D illustrated style, no distortion.

## Clip 1: The hook ("Okay y'all, let me tell you somethin'")
She lifts her head off her hand, leans toward the camera with a big smile, and talks to the camera like she's telling her best friend some news. She gestures with her free hand. Camera slowly pushes in.

## Clip 2: The sigh ("I got a LOT on my plate right now")
She gives a playful dramatic sigh, puts her hand on her chest, shakes her head, and laughs. Coffee mug still in her other hand. Camera stays still.

## Clip 3: Standing up
She sets her coffee mug down on the desk, stands up from her chair, smooths her pink blazer, and smiles confidently at the camera. Camera slowly tilts up to follow her.

## Clip 4: Walking ("Cozy. Cute. And it's tellin' the truth")
She walks out from behind the desk toward the camera with confident steps, smiling, ponytail swinging. Camera slowly moves backward in front of her.

## Clip 5: The call to action ("Grab yours, sis. Link in my bio!")
She points down toward the bottom of the screen, then winks and blows a kiss at the camera. Camera stays still.

## Clip 6 (bonus): Wearing the tee
Use this prompt on the same picture: she is now wearing a red Christmas t-shirt instead of her pink blazer, holding a plate of Christmas cookies, laughing and taking a bite of a gingerbread cookie.
(Tip: if the app lets you add a second image, add a frame of the cookie tee so the shirt design matches.)

## Adding your voice
- **Kling AI** has a **Lip Sync** feature: open your finished clip, choose Lip Sync, and upload your voice recording. It makes her mouth match your words.
- Lip sync works best on clips where she faces the camera and talks: Clips 1, 2 and 5.
- Keep each voice piece about as long as the clip (5 seconds or less).

## Tips
- If the face changes, regenerate. It often takes 2–3 tries to get a good one.
- Shorter, simpler prompts give cleaner movement. One action per clip.
- Download each clip you like and send them over to be stitched into the final reel with captions, the tee video and your voice.
