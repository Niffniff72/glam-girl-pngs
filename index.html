How the comment keyword works

  • Set a comment-growth tool (ManyChat, Sendible, or Instagram's own "automatic replies") to watch for the keyword — e.g. GLAM, CATMOM, or TUMBLER — on a specific post or Reel.
  • When someone comments the keyword, the tool auto-DMs them a link to the freebie or a discount code, and can auto-reply on the comment too, so it's visible social proof.
  • Rotate the keyword per launch (new drop = new keyword) so you can track which posts are actually driving DMs and sales.
  • Full DM + comment-reply scripts are in content/marketing-kit.md — copy, paste, and plug into your automation tool.
