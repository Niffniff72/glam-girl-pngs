Pro tips

    
    
      
  • AI tools make low-resolution images. Upscale to the pixel size in your print notes before you send it to a vendor.
    • 
      
  • For DTF and UV DTF, remove the white background in Canva or a background remover and save as a transparent PNG.
    • 
      
  • Flat a little? Reply: "Add more shine to the hair, clothes and accessories only. Keep the skin smooth and natural."
    • 
      
  • Logo snuck in? Reply: "Remove all logos and brand marks. Make the shoes and bag plain."
    • 
      
  • Check every word for spelling before you list it.
