## Setup notes

- Swap every bracketed `[LINK]` / `[DATE]` placeholder with your real Payhip URLs and dates before turning automations on.
- Instagram enforces a delay before a new comment-to-DM automation can message people who haven't messaged the account first — Meta requires the person to comment (which counts as opt-in) before the auto-DM sends, so keep the keyword rule scoped to the specific post you're promoting.
- Rotate keywords per launch so it's obvious in your automation tool's analytics which post drove the most DMs → sales.
