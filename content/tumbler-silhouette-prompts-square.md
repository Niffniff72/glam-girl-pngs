# Silhouette Tumbler Prompts: 20 oz Square Version

Every prompt below already has the **20 oz tumbler shape (9:8)**, a **thin gold outline** around the silhouette, and **text stacked on lines**. Copy and paste.

**Habits that keep results clean:**
- Start a **new Gemini chat for each design**, so designs don't blend together.
- If it comes out wide anyway, reply: *"Make this exact design a square image, 9:8 ratio. Move the woman and the text closer together, with more glitter above and below."*
- Check **spelling** and that nothing important touches the **left or right edge** (the seam).
- Midjourney: add `--ar 9:8` to the end.
