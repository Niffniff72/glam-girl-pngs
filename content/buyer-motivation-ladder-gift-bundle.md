## STEP 5: Content

### 3 conversational hooks
1. "Everybody asks me how I get my candles to smell this strong… so I made a whole gift box around one."
2. "If you're stuck on what to get the woman who prays for everybody, I have an idea."
3. "This is what I'd want to unwrap at Christmas, and I'm not even exaggerating."

### 3 emotionally driven angles
1. **The gift you don't have to second-guess:** strong throw, professional finish, and a maker who replaces anything that isn't right
2. **For the women who keep us grounded:** moms, grandmothers, church mothers, first ladies
3. **Faith in the everyday:** morning coffee, a quiet prayer, a candle lit while company comes in

### Mini movie 1: "What Is That Smell?" 🕯️ (30–45 seconds)
- **Scene 1:** Saturday afternoon. A woman sets her dining table and lights a candle as the doorbell rings.
- **Scene 2:** Her sister walks in, stops in the doorway, and sniffs. "Okay… what is that?"
- **Scene 3:** They sit down. The sister keeps glancing at the candle, then picks it up and reads the label.
- **Scene 4:** At the end of the visit, the host hands her a wrapped gift box. "I got you one too."
- **Scene 5:** The sister lifts the lid: candle, tumbler, journal. A small smile, no speech.
- **On-screen text:** *Dream Gal Haven Candles · link in bio*

### Mini movie 2: "Her Turn" 💜 (45–60 seconds)
- **Scene 1:** A young woman wraps a gift box at the kitchen table while on the phone: "Yes, Grandma, I'm coming Sunday."
- **Scene 2:** Sunday. Grandma opens the box and runs her finger over the gold cross on the journal.
- **Scene 3:** That week, Grandma has morning coffee in the tumbler, writes in the journal, and lights the candle.
- **Scene 4:** Weeks later at church, Grandma hands a church sister a box just like it. The granddaughter, sitting beside her, catches her eye and smiles.
- **On-screen text:** *Some gifts get passed on. · Blessed & Highly Favored gift set*
